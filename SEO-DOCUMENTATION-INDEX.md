## 💡 Quick Tips for Success

### ✅ DO's

- Write naturally for users first
- Use keywords 2-3 times per page
- Include internal links strategically
- Add descriptive alt text to images
- Monitor analytics weekly
- Update content regularly
- Respond to reviews

### ❌ DON'Ts

- Never keyword stuff
- Avoid duplicate content
- Don't hide text/links
- Avoid cloaking or deception
- Never ignore broken links
- Skip A/B testing descriptions
- Ignore analytics data
